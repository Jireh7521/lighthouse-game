Hunger drains as you run.

  • Above 60 you mend a heart every few seconds.
  • 25 to 60 nothing changes: you are steady.
  • Below 25 no healing, and your legs get heavy.
  • At 0 you lose a heart every 9 seconds.

Fruit from the trees fills it. The spring in the cave fills and heals in one go.
